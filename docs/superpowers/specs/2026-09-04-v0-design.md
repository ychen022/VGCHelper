# Approved V0 design

Approved in conversation on 2026-09-04. Implement two local MCP coaching workflows over shared deterministic tools, using the existing TypeScript prototype.

## Requirements

- Node.js 22 or newer; TypeScript, SQLite, stdio MCP; preserve local history.
- Target Pokemon Champions VGC 2026 Regulation M-B through explicit versioned profiles. Do not identify the broad legacy Champions format as M-B without evidence.
- Import log, JSON and downloaded HTML replays; retain raw events and before/after turn states.
- Distinguish known, inferred and unknown information, including open team sheets and rounded public HP. No later-turn information in earlier decisions.
- Replay coaching cites consequential turns, reviews joint actions and plausible responses, supplies state-aware damage scenarios, and ends with up to three practice priorities.
- Evaluate 15 user leads against up to 12 distinct representative complete M-B teams, with bounded opening scenarios, bring-four reasoning and targeted changes with tradeoffs.
- Pin calculator mechanics and source snapshots in each report. Scores and hypothesis confidence are heuristics, never calibrated win probabilities.
- Keep source access cached and attributed; preserve previous valid snapshots on refresh failure.
- Save team identity with reports and separate replay trends by regulation and team version.
- Validate parser/calculation edge cases and MCP workflows; attempt public replay and source smoke checks. Clearly distinguish synthetic tests from real-game validation.

## Boundaries

V0 is decision support. Full battle trees, ladder automation, model training and best-of-three adaptation are deferred. Unsupported interactions must be exposed as limitations rather than silently treated as exact simulation. The local folder is not a Git repository; do not initialize or overwrite repository metadata merely for this work.
