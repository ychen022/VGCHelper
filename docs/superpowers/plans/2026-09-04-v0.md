**Goal:** Deliver usable local replay-coach and team-builder MCP workflows with auditable evidence and conservative uncertainty.

**Architecture:** Extend the existing services and SQLite records. Share a state-aware Champions calculator; keep bounded scenario analysis separate from protocol reconstruction and agent prompts.

**Tech Stack:** Node.js 22+, TypeScript, Vitest, SQLite, MCP, vendored Smogon calculator.

**Spec:** ../specs/2026-09-04-v0-design.md
